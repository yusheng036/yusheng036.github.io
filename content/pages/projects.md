## MBTA Bus Crowding Analysis

An analysis of historical MBTA ridership data that compares eight machine-learning models for predicting bus crowding across routes, stops, and time periods. Random Forest achieved the strongest results and was used to identify the busiest routes and travel periods.

**Members:** [Yu Sheng Aow](https://github.com/yusheng036), [Dave Feng](https://github.com/Davidioxide), [Yucheng Xue](https://github.com/RoyeeXue), [Cindy Lin](https://github.com/lllllllchh), and [Gor Matcakian](https://github.com/matcakian)

## Proximo — Identity-Based AI Tutors

Proximo is an HCI research prototype that investigates whether an AI tutor framed as a learner's successful future self can improve motivation and engagement. In an eight-participant pilot study, the future-self tutor produced higher engagement, trust, and enjoyment than a standard AI tutor, with participants sending 20% more messages on average.

**Creator:** [Yu Sheng Aow](https://github.com/yusheng036)
